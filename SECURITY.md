# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability, please do not report it through a public GitHub issue. Instead, email [security@selemis.com](mailto:security@selemis.com) with sufficient information to reproduce and assess the issue.

We will review complete, credible reports and aim to acknowledge them within five business days. Where appropriate, we will provide an initial assessment or status update within ten business days.

Reports that are automated, unverifiable, duplicative, or lack meaningful reproduction details may not receive an individual response.
